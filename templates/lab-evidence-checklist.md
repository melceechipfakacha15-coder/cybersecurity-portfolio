# Lab Evidence Checklist

Use this checklist before marking a portfolio project complete.

## Technical evidence

- [ ] The scenario and investigation question are clear.
- [ ] The lab environment can be reproduced.
- [ ] Commands, queries, filters, or event IDs are included.
- [ ] Screenshots support a specific finding rather than decorating the page.
- [ ] Timestamps, time zones, hosts, and accounts are explained.
- [ ] Confirmed facts are separated from assumptions.
- [ ] The severity rating is justified.
- [ ] Response and escalation decisions follow from the evidence.

## Writing quality

- [ ] The executive summary is understandable without reading every technical step.
- [ ] Acronyms are explained on first use.
- [ ] The investigation follows a logical timeline.
- [ ] Claims are precise and supported.
- [ ] Recommendations are practical and prioritized.
- [ ] The lessons-learned section is honest and specific.

## Safety

- [ ] The work was performed in an authorized lab or simulation.
- [ ] No real credentials, tokens, private keys, or cookies are present.
- [ ] Personal and organizational information is sanitized.
- [ ] Raw logs and packet captures are excluded unless generated solely for the lab and reviewed carefully.
- [ ] Every staged file has been inspected before commit.
